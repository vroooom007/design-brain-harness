# Curated Research Sources (Stage 2 Scouts)

Use these starting points to gather **real, live shipped websites and product patterns**, not conceptual mockups.

## Marketing Websites, Hero Sections & Editorial Craft
- **Godly (`godly.website`)**: High-craft editorial, typography-led, and motion-rich live websites. Filter by style/type.
- **Land-book (`land-book.com`)**: Clean real-world landing pages across SaaS, agencies, and products.
- **SiteInspire (`siteinspire.com`)**: Strong editorial grids, restrained minimalism, and non-SaaS visual languages (great for escaping tech-landing-page homogeneity).
- **Minimal Gallery (`minimal.gallery`)**: Restraint, whitespace rhythm, and sharp typographic hierarchy.
- **Awesome DESIGN.md (`github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md`)**: Decoded real-world brand visual anatomy (Apple, Stripe, Linear, Sanity, Runway, etc.) in readable Markdown.

## Product UI, Flows & Information Architecture
- **Mobbin (`mobbin.com`)**: Real shipped web and mobile product screens, onboarding flows, settings, empty states, and navigation patterns.
- **Screenlane / Refero (`refero.design`)**: Real web app UI patterns organized by component and flow.
- **Component Gallery (`component.gallery`)**: Cross-company reference of how mature design systems solve real UI components.

## Micro-Interactions & Motion References
- **ReactBits (`reactbits.dev`)**: Selective interactive components and kinetic details (use sparingly; never turn a page into a tech demo).
- **Rauno Freiberg / Emil Kowalski / Linear / Stripe Docs**: Benchmarks for restrained, physically grounded interaction craft.

## What to Treat With Caution
- **Dribbble / Behance conceptual shots:** Often look pretty in a 4:3 crop but fail on real copy lengths, mobile responsiveness, accessibility, and actual UX conversion. Prefer **live shipped URLs** you can inspect and scroll.
- **Competitor clone traps:** Inspect 1-2 direct competitors specifically to list what *not* to copy so your design stands apart in the category.
