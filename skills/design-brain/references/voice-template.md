## 4. Contextual UX Tone Matrix (Same Voice, Adapted to User State)

| Surface / Moment | User's Mindset | Tone & Concrete Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero & Headlines** | Skeptical, scanning in 3 seconds | Concrete outcome, zero fluff. Say what it actually does. |
| **Subcopy & Proof** | Looking for evidence | Specific mechanisms, real limits, plain verbs. |
| **Primary CTAs** | Deciding whether to click | Verb + concrete result (e.g., *"Start a sandbox"* instead of *"Get Started"*). |
| **Onboarding / Empty States** | Oriented, needs first action | One clear next step, no cheerleading. |
| **Errors & Edge Cases** | Frustrated, blocked | Plain cause + exact fix. Zero fake apologies (*"Oops! Something went wrong"*). |
